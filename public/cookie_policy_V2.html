COOKIE POLICY

Last updated October 03, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Pass Professional Qualifications Limited, trading as GetChartered (we, us, our), uses cookies and similar technologies on getchartered.app (the Website). It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage and session storage, work in the same way and are covered by this policy when we say "cookies".

2. OUR APPROACH

We only use cookies and similar technologies that are needed to provide the Website and the features you ask for, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your settings. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies on the Website. Because of this, we do not show a cookie consent banner. If we add any non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Fonts used on the Website are served from our own domain, not loaded from a third-party font service. Sign-in is provided by Auth0 and card payments by Stripe; when you use those pages you leave our Website and they may set their own cookies, which are governed by their own policies. Payment card details are entered only on Stripe's page and are never seen or stored by us.

5. HOW TO CONTROL COOKIES

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; instructions for each browser are available on its help pages and at aboutcookies.org. If you block strictly necessary cookies you will not be able to sign in, and some parts of the Website will not work.

6. THE MOBILE APP

This policy covers the Website. How the GetChartered mobile app handles your information, including any technologies it uses on your device, is described in our Privacy Policy.
